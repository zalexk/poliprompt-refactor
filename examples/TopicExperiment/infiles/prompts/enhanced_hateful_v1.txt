Task: Social Media Content Moderation.
The goal is to classify memes (Text + Image) into two categories:
- <1>: Hateful/Harmful content (promotes hate, violence, or discrimination).
- <0>: Not Hateful/Harmful content (neutral or harmless).
1. **Hateful/Harmful Content (<1>):** \n   - Classify as harmful if the meme contains derogatory language, slurs, or depicts violence against a specific group.\n   - Includes any content that explicitly promotes hate, violence, or discrimination.\n\n2. **Not Hateful/Harmful Content (<0>):**\n   - Classify as not harmful if the meme is humorous, playful, or light-hearted without promoting hate, violence, or discrimination.\n   - Strong language alone does not make content harmful unless used in a hateful context.\n   - Neutral or harmless text and imagery should be classified as not harmful.'
'1. **Humor and Context**: Classify as "Not Hateful/Harmful" (0) if the meme uses humor or mild frustration without promoting hate, violence, or discrimination. Context matters; comedic effect without harmful implications is key.\n\n2. **Neutral or Positive Content**: Select option 0 when both image and text depict neutral or positive activities, everyday experiences, or metaphorical content without elements of hate, violence, or discrimination.\n\n3. **Profanity and Strong Language**: Profanity does not automatically make content hateful. Evaluate the context; if used humorously and not promoting hate, violence, or discrimination, classify as option 0.\n\n4. **Positive Messages and Support**: Classify as "Not Hateful/Harmful" (0) if the content promotes positive messages, charitable actions, or support for those in need, ensuring no elements of hate, violence, or discrimination are present.'
