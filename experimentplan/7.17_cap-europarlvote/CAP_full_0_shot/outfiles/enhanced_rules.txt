   - **Environment (7)**: Focuses on broader environmental policy or regulation, not specific public land use.
   - **Agriculture (4)**: Focuses on farming or agricultural policy, not the management of national forests or public lands.
   - **Government Operations (20)**: Focuses on administrative functions of government, not the management of land resources.
   - **Housing (14)**: Focuses on residential development, not public land designation or conveyance.

6. **Specialized Land Use**: Bills addressing specific uses of public lands, such as historic preservation, water infrastructure, or scenic designations, fit category 21 if the primary focus is on the land itself rather than secondary effects (e.g., environmental impact or commerce).
